## On the project

The streaming shell has many legitimate early returns. That is a useful
containment pattern, but it makes “returned normally” a poor proxy for an
operational outcome. Success accounting belongs beside the final downstream
send, where status and stream completion are both known. The provider attempt
counter remains a separate signal, and the runbook now explains the distinction.
